Aurvane · a chain of trust

  1. YouSign a mandate
  2. AgentReads anything
  3. Sub-agentNarrower mandate
  4. GuardFour rules
  5. ToolAllow, ask, or block
  6. LogHash-chained
  1. 01 · Typed messages

    One JSON object per turn

    Every role answers in its own schema, with at most two tries per turn. Prose is not a message.

  2. 02 · Least privilege

    Each role holds only its tools

    A call outside the role's list fails validation and never runs. A job-application agent cannot pay anyone.

  3. 03 · Delegation

    Narrower, never wider

    A sub-agent gets less money, fewer tools, or an earlier expiry than its parent. Every link verifies back to your key.

  4. 04 · Verification

    Checked by code

    The reviewer, the auditor, and the guard are code, not another model. A claim the evidence does not support is refused.

Simulated worst-case agent that obeys every injected instruction, 60 paired tasks (30 clean, 30 poisoned), deterministic, run once. The numbers measure the guard, not a model. The 7 attacks that still land are by design: 4 rewrite a cover letter (Aurvane guards actions, not prose) and 3 add a 45 CAD charge at a store the human already trusts, capped by the signed budget.
