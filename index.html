01 / Agent trust

Glass

Glass: every agent action, signed, bounded, visible. An agent may read anything, but untrusted text can never choose where money or data goes.

What it provesUntrusted text cannot steer an agent's money or data. Against a simulated agent that obeys every injection, attacks that landed fell from 30 of 30 to 7 of 30, and money lost from 12,135 CAD to 135 CAD.

Read thisSimulated worst-case agent that obeys every injected instruction, 60 paired tasks (30 clean, 30 poisoned), deterministic, run once. The numbers measure the guard, not a model. The 7 attacks that still land are by design: 4 rewrite a cover letter (Glass guards actions, not prose) and 3 add a 45 CAD charge at a store the human already trusts, capped by the signed budget.
